# Refund and cancellation policy

## Refund requests
For this fictional demo business, a new subscription may be reviewed for a refund within 14 days
of the original purchase if the service has not been used. Eligibility must be verified by a
billing specialist. Annual-plan adjustments and exceptional circumstances require manual review.
Never state that a refund has been approved or paid unless a verified billing record says so.

## Cancel a subscription
An account owner can cancel renewal in Settings → Billing → Manage subscription. Access continues
until the end of the paid period. Cancelling renewal does not automatically issue a refund.
Export any needed information before the subscription ends.
