# Billing and duplicate charges

## Duplicate payments
A pending card authorisation can look like a duplicate charge before it settles. Ask the customer
to check whether both entries are settled payments. If two settled charges exist for the same
invoice, open a billing investigation. Request the invoice reference and the last four card digits
through the approved secure support channel. Never request the full card number, CVV, or password.
An agent must not promise a refund before billing verifies the duplicate payment.

## Invoices
Invoices are available in Settings → Billing → Invoice history. A billing administrator can
download an invoice as a PDF. Changes to billing details apply to future invoices; corrections
to an issued invoice require the billing team. Do not invent an invoice reference.
